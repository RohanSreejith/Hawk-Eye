# SAHAYI — 30-Hour Hackathon Presentation Demo Script

## The Core Product Principle
> **The product is NOT:** "YOLO detecting construction workers."  
> **The product is:** CCTV $\rightarrow$ Detection $\rightarrow$ Safety Event $\rightarrow$ Risk Assessment $\rightarrow$ Local Intervention $\rightarrow$ Supervisor Escalation $\rightarrow$ Evidence $\rightarrow$ Incident Management $\rightarrow$ Site Intelligence.

---

## 7-Step Hackathon Pitch & Live Demo Walkthrough

### STEP 1: Open Command Center (`http://localhost:5173/`)
- **Opening Narrative:**
  > *"Construction sites already have CCTV, but CCTV mostly acts as a passive recording system. It only tells you what happened after an injury occurred. It doesn't tell the right person to act before it's too late. SAHAYI transforms existing CCTV into an active safety system."*
- **Visuals:**
  - Show the dark industrial Command Center with 5 camera nodes streaming live.
  - Show the KPI header: Cameras Online (5/5), Active Incidents, and Alerts Suppressed by Deduplication.
  - Highlight the Privacy Badge: *"Video analysis is performed strictly for site safety. Anonymous temporary IDs only — zero facial recognition."*

---

### STEP 2: Trigger Scenario 1 — No Helmet / PPE Violation
- **Action:**
  - Open `/demo` tab or click **Trigger No Helmet**.
  - Switch to `/kiosk` (Worker Safety Kiosk).
- **Demo Moment:**
  - Kiosk flashes Amber Warning: `SAFETY WARNING: NO HELMET DETECTED | ZONE: MAIN GATE`.
  - Browser SpeechSynthesis speaks aloud: *"Safety warning! Dispatched voice alert to site kiosk; halt entry until approved helmet issued!"*
  - Command Center logs incident with risk score 42/100.

---

### STEP 3: Trigger Scenario 2 — Vehicle / Worker Proximity Hazard
- **Action:**
  - Click **Trigger Vehicle Proximity**.
- **Demo Moment:**
  - Top emergency banner flashes: `CRITICAL SAFETY EVENT: VEHICLE–PERSON PROXIMITY`.
  - Supervisor Mobile (`/mobile`) immediately receives a high-priority dispatch with audio alert.
  - 10-second forensic video evidence clip is automatically rendered with telemetry bounding boxes and hazard distance lines.

---

### STEP 4: Inspect Forensic Incident Detail
- **Action:**
  - Click **[PLAY EVIDENCE & DISPATCH]** on the emergency banner.
- **Narrative:**
  - Show the 10-second MP4 evidence playback (5s before + 5s after).
  - Point to the AI summary generated by Ollama / Analyst Agent.
  - Walk through the audit timeline:
    - `11:32:14` Detection
    - `11:32:15` Risk Engine Confirmed High Severity (87/100)
    - `11:32:15` Kiosk Warning Triggered
    - `11:32:16` Supervisor Escalated
    - `11:32:20` Forensic Clip Generated
- Click **[ACKNOWLEDGE ALERT]** $\rightarrow$ Status updates across all screens via WebSockets!

---

### STEP 5: Open Site Risk Heatmap
- **Action:**
  - Scroll to the SVG Site Risk Heatmap.
- **Narrative:**
  - Click on **Loading Zone** and **Crane Area**.
  - Show how spatial incident history surfaces recurring hotspots for EHS managers before shift meetings.

---

### STEP 6: Open Agent Transparency Pipeline
- **Action:**
  - Expand the **AI Response Pipeline & Agent Transparency** drawer.
- **Narrative:**
  - Show the live operational decisions:
    - *Perception Agent* normalized coordinates.
    *Safety Agent* evaluated rule thresholds.
    *Risk Agent* computed the heuristic 0-100 score.
    *Analyst Agent* drafted the forensic explanation.
    *Response Agent* dispatched kiosk audio and mobile notifications.

---

### STEP 7: Final Punchline
> *"The system didn't just detect the incident. It brought the incident to the person who can act on it."*
